How CivicSaathi Works

A transparent 4-step digital workflow connecting Pune citizens directly to field officers

1. Capture & Lodge

Select Pune department, take a photo or upload evidence, tag your Pune ward and landmark.

2. Digital Ref ID

Get an instant unique reference number (`CS-PUN-2026-XXXX`) and digital acknowledgement slip with QR code.

3. Department Action

Assigned PMC or State Officer inspects the site, updates status, and logs investigation remarks.

4. Redressal & Closure

Problem resolved with completion remarks and evidence photograph. Citizen views full audit trail.
